# Resolute Raccoon: Ubuntu's Rebirth under the Shield of Rust and AI

The release of **Ubuntu 26.04 LTS**, codenamed **"Resolute Raccoon"**, is not just another update on Canonical's calendar. It is, in essence, a statement of principles about the future of computing. In a world where cyber threats have become industrialized and artificial intelligence has moved from promise to infrastructure necessity, Ubuntu has chosen to rebuild its foundations.

## The Oxidation Project: Armoring the Core
The most daring innovation in this version is undoubtedly the **Oxidation Project**. Aware that more than 70% of modern security vulnerabilities stem from memory management errors in C, Canonical has launched a massive migration toward **Rust**.

This transition is not cosmetic. Critical tools like `sudo-rs` and the `coreutils` (basic commands like `ls` or `cp`) now operate under the memory safety provided by Rust. The result is an operating system that drastically reduces its attack surface by design, positioning itself as the preferred choice for infrastructures that cannot afford a single point of failure.

## Artificial Intelligence: Native Power on the Desktop
Ubuntu 26.04 breaks the barrier between hardware and AI software. For the first time, official repositories include native support for development kits from the big three in silicon: **NVIDIA CUDA**, **AMD ROCm**, and **Intel oneAPI**.

No longer is it necessary to navigate labyrinths of third-party drivers. With a simple command, developers have access to an environment optimized for local model training and inference. But AI in "Resolute Raccoon" is not just for experts; the system introduces **agentic workflows** in the terminal and on the desktop, allowing AI to assist in complex tasks like log debugging or network configuration, always under a model of data sovereignty and local processing.

## A Generational Leap in User Experience
The desktop, powered by **GNOME 50**, is the smoothest to date. With an exclusive transition to **Wayland**, Ubuntu embraces modern technologies like **HDR** and **Variable Refresh Rate (VRR)** in a stable way. New system applications, such as *Resources* (written in Rust), allow for monitoring not only CPU but also the usage of Neural Processing Units (**NPUs**), reflecting the new reality of modern hardware.

## Conclusion: A Tool for the Next Decade
With the **Linux 7.0 Kernel** as its engine and a support commitment of up to 15 years, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS stands as a bastion of stability and cutting edge. While removing some classic tools may require a brief learning curve, the net benefit in security, performance, and AI capabilities establishes "Resolute Raccoon" as the new gold standard for professionals and businesses alike.
